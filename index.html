Our Story

What started as a simple neighbourly connection during the unexpected days of COVID soon blossomed into a beautiful friendship. Through shared conversations, laughter and getting to know one another, we discovered something more than just friendship. Our paths crossed as neighbours, but our hearts brought us together. And what began as a friendship during an uncertain time turned into a love story we get to continue writing together, forever. ❤️
